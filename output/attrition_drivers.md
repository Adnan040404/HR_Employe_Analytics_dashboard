# What goes with leaving

Company-wide attrition is 16.1%. **Lift** is a group's rate divided by that. The interval is a 95% Wilson interval: the wider it is, the less you should read into the rate. Only groups of 50+ people are ranked.

This describes who left in this dataset. It doesn't show *why* people left, and several of these factors overlap (junior people are also younger, lower paid and newer).
